I remember the first time I thought I was being super efficient in my preprocessing pipeline.

I scaled my entire dataset, then split it into train and test. My model metrics looked great, almost *too* good. It took a while to realize I'd introduced a subtle, silent data leak.

When you scale your entire dataset before splitting, information from your test set (like min/max or mean/std) gets incorporated into the scaling parameters. Your model then learns from test data it shouldn't have seen. It's like peeking at the answer key!

This is especially tricky in biological data. Small leaks can lead to big overestimations of performance. Always split your data first, then fit your scaler *only* on the training set.
