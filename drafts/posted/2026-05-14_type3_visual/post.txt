I remember the early days when I first started coding... my `base` environment was a tangled mess! It was like a digital junk drawer.

I'd just `pip install` anything and everything. Then, a few months later, I'd start a new project, and suddenly nothing worked. Dependency conflicts everywhere! It felt like trying to make two different recipes in the same mixing bowl.

It took me a while to learn that keeping projects isolated is crucial. A dedicated `conda` environment for each project (or even specific analyses) prevents version nightmares and ensures reproducibility. Future you will thank current you!
