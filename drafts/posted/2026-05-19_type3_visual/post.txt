I remember a time when my Python setup was a complete mess. Dependencies clashing, scripts breaking for no clear reason – it was frustrating to debug!

Pip-installing everything into your base environment feels easy at first. But trust me, you're setting yourself up for 'dependency hell.' One project needs an older `pandas` version, another needs the newest `scikit-learn`, and suddenly nothing works. I've been there, pulling my hair out trying to fix conflicts.

Using a dedicated Conda environment for *each* project isolates its dependencies. It's like giving each project its own clean workspace. Your proteomics analysis won't break your single-cell pipeline, and vice-versa. Plus, it makes your work reproducible for others (and future you!).
