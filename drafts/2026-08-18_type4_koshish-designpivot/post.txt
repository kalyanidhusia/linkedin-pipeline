Ever started building something, only to realize the best path wasn't the first one you imagined? 

That's been my journey with `pxEnrich`. We initially thought about text-based enrichment and leveraging LLMs to parse context. Sounded smart, right?

But as we dug deeper, we saw the real power in grounding our insights in source evidence from metadata. The pivot was clear: move from just parsing text to building AI assertions qualified by biological rules, then validating at the proteome level. It’s a much more robust approach.

The human element, with expert adjudication, is still the gold standard to ensure our evidence-qualified metadata truly makes sense. It's a reminder that even 'smart machines' need our biological intuition to get it right.
