I was doing exploratory clustering on some proteomics data last month — nothing fancy, just k-means to see if tumor samples grouped by subtype.

Ran the notebook. Got three clean clusters. Took a screenshot for the lab meeting.

Reran it the next morning to add one more plot.

Completely different clusters.

I spent 20 minutes convinced I'd corrupted the data file. Then I realized: no random seed. K-means initialization is stochastic. My "discovery" was just... random noise with a different starting point.

Now I put `np.random.seed(42)` at the top of every notebook, even the messy 2am ones I think I'll delete tomorrow. 

Because the only thing worse than irreproducible science is irreproducible science that you did to yourself.

Future-you will thank present-you.
