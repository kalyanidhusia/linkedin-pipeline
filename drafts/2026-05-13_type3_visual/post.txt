Just last week, I was chatting with a student about their new ML model, and we hit a snag with performance on a validation set. It reminded me of a super common trap I've fallen into myself!

It's so easy to normalize your whole dataset upfront. I've done it! But when you do, your test set or validation set 'peeks' at the training data's distribution. This is called data leakage.

This 'leakage' makes your model look fantastic on paper (99% accuracy, wow!), but it will disappoint you in the real world when it sees truly new, unseen data. We want our models to be honest about their performance.

The trick? Fit your `StandardScaler` (or `MinMaxScaler`) *only* on your training data. Then, use that *same* fitted scaler to transform *both* your training and test sets. Simple, but crucial for honest model evaluation.
